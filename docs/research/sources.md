# Verified sources used in Phases 18–19

The following public sources were accessed and read on 2026-09-15. They support background and design claims only; they do not supply project-specific performance results.

1. Canadian Institute for Cybersecurity, University of New Brunswick. The official CIC-IDS2017 page describes the labeled network-flow dataset, its five-day capture period, attack categories, and CICFlowMeter-derived features: <https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ids-2017.html>.
2. Morris Dworkin, NIST Special Publication 800-38D, *Recommendation for Block Cipher Modes of Operation: Galois/Counter Mode (GCM) and GMAC*. The publication defines GCM as authenticated encryption with associated data: <https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/38/d/final>.
3. A. Langley, M. Hamburg, and S. Turner, RFC 7748, *Elliptic Curves for Security*. Section 6.1 describes X25519 in an ECDH protocol and deriving a symmetric key from the shared secret: <https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7748.html>.
4. S. Josefsson and I. Liusvaara, RFC 8032, *EdDSA: Ed25519 and Ed448*. The RFC specifies Ed25519 and discusses deterministic signatures and signature properties: <https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8032.html>.

No citation in the report or paper is used to claim a project result. Project-specific numbers are taken only from the repository's previously executed benchmark outputs and are labeled with their measurement scope.
